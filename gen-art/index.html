Botanical Compositions

An interactive composition tool that arranges fruits, flowers, and leaves over a randomly chosen color palette. Each New Art click reshuffles position, scale, and color. Pick a configuration you like and save your art as an image.

Built with p5.js. Palettes are drawn from a curated JSON set; botanical motifs are composed as image overlays against a generated background.
