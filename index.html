01 / Product Design Designing Digital Products for Women's health

Your Upgrade
by Dr. Colleen

UX Research UI Design Design System React Claude Conversational AI

A mobile experience featuring an AI-powered conversational assistant for women navigating Peri + Menopause, a major life transition that remains understudied, stigmatized, and poorly supported by existing digital tools.

This project spans the full product journey, from user research to a live application developed using Anthropic’s Claude and designed end-to-end in collaboration with Colleen Fogarty Draper, PhD, RD. The system translates user data into personalized nutrition and lifestyle guidance through a hybrid AI–human model.

Built around trust, the AI Assistant serves as a transparent intermediary, delivering evidence-based recommendations across 20+ menopause-related symptoms while adapting its tone and support to each user’s needs and emotional state.

04 / About
